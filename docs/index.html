Brief

**C0** (alive) — After the material has been encoded, practicing retrieval produces more durable retention than spending the same time restudying. The advantage grows as the delay to the real test grows. The claim does not hold when encoding never succeeded, when the practice test is a poor match to what must later be remembered, or when “testing” is high-stakes performance rather than practice.
